06 / COLLECTIONS & ACCOUNTS

Three financial views. Keep them separate.

Customer freight, courier settlement and CargoNest subscription revenue are different ledgers.

1 · CUSTOMER FREIGHT

Customer charge → Paid / To Pay / Credit / FOC → Actual collections → Outstanding → Refund follow-up

2 · COURIER ACCOUNT

Provider shipment cost → Partner payable → Settlement → Remaining partner balance

3 · PLATFORM SUBSCRIPTION

Company plan payment → Subscription validity → CargoNest platform revenue
